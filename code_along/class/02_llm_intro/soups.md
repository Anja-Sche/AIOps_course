**1. Vietnamese Phở Gà (Chicken Pho)**  
A fragrant, clear broth simmered with charred ginger, star anise, and cinnamon, served over soft rice noodles with tender chicken and fresh herb garnishes.  
- Chicken bones or carcass  
- Star anise, cinnamon stick, cloves  
- Charred ginger and onion  
- Fish sauce, rock sugar  
- Rice noodles, boneless chicken thighs  
- Fresh Thai basil, cilantro, lime wedges, bean sprouts  

**How to make it:**  
1. Roast ginger and onion over an open flame or broiler until blackened, then add to a pot with chicken bones and spices.  
2. Simmer gently for 1–2 hours, skimming impurities, then strain and season with fish sauce and sugar.  
3. Cook rice noodles according to package directions, divide into bowls, top with sliced chicken, and ladle hot broth over.  
4. Garnish generously with Thai basil, cilantro, bean sprouts, and a squeeze of lime.  

**2. Thai Tom Yum Goong (Hot & Sour Shrimp Soup)**  
A vibrant, aromatic soup balancing spicy chilies, tangy lime, and earthy lemongrass and galangal, packed with succulent shrimp and mushrooms.  
- Fresh jumbo shrimp, peeled  
- Lemongrass stalks, galangal root, kaffir lime leaves  
- Thai bird’s eye chilies, smashed  
- Lime juice, fish sauce, palm sugar  
- Straw or shiitake mushrooms  
- Coriander roots or cilantro stems  

**How to make it:**  
1. Bruise and lightly crush lemongrass, galangal, and kaffir lime leaves, then add to a pot of water and bring to a boil.  
2. Add chilies, mushrooms, and shrimp; cook just until the shrimp turns opaque and pink.  
3. Remove from heat, stir in fresh lime juice and fish sauce, then adjust with palm sugar to balance sour, salty, and spicy notes.  
4. Garnish with cilantro stems and serve piping hot with jasmine rice or as a standalone soup.  

**3. Japanese Misoshiru (Miso Soup)**  
A daily staple of light, umami-rich broth made from fermented miso and dashi, featuring silky tofu and toasty nori for a quick, comforting bowl.  
- Dashi powder or kombu (kelp) + katsuobushi (bonito flakes)  
- White miso paste  
- Firm tofu, cubed  
- Dried nori sheets, torn into pieces  
- Green onions, thinly sliced  

**How to make it:**  
1. Dissolve dashi in 4 cups of water, bring to a gentle simmer (do not boil vigorously).  
2. Add tofu cubes and cook for 2–3 minutes until warmed through.  
3. In a separate small bowl, whisk miso with a ladle of hot broth until smooth, then gently stir it back into the pot – avoid boiling miso to preserve flavor and probiotics.  
4. Top with torn nori and sliced green onions, serve immediately in small bowls.
